PauseCard – Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Xinyu Zhang, developer of PauseCard

Summary

PauseCard collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server run by the developer. Everything the app learns about you (which episodes you watched, where you paused, which words you marked as known) stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Learning cards (the analysed subtitle text of episodes you played) and your playback position.
  • Learn-mode sessions: the lines you paused on, the words you tapped, dictionary links you opened, words you marked as known.
  • Your settings.

You can delete all of this by deleting the app. Reports you export (HTML, CSV, Anki) are files you create and share yourself.

Network connections the app makes

  • mediathekviewweb.de – the open index of the German public broadcasters' media libraries, to find episodes and their subtitle files. The request contains the series name or your search text.
  • zdf.de, ardmediathek.de and their content delivery networks – to stream the video and download the subtitle file of the episode you chose. These are the broadcasters' own services and subject to their privacy policies.
  • dict.cc, dwds.de, translate.google.com – only when you tap a dictionary link on the card; the word is opened in your browser.

No request identifies you beyond what any web request carries (your IP address, which the respective service sees). The developer receives nothing.

On-device processing

Language analysis uses Apple's NaturalLanguage framework and rules built into the app. The translation line (English or Chinese, your choice) is produced by Apple's on-device Translation framework (iOS 18 or later); iOS may ask you once to download the German and the translation language packs. No subtitle text is sent to any translation service.

Safari extension

The included Safari extension runs only on pages you allow it for (zdf.de, ardmediathek.de, netflix.com). It reads the page's subtitle track to build the learning card, stores the result on your device and sends nothing anywhere else.

In-app purchase

The free trial and the one-time purchase of PauseCard Full are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. The app receives from Apple only whether your Apple ID owns the trial or the full version and the date the trial started; it never sees your name, payment details or address. Refunds and receipts are managed by Apple.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
