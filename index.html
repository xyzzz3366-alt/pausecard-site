PauseCard – Support

Learn German from ZDF and ARD series, on iPhone and iPad

PauseCard plays the German public broadcasters' media libraries with two subtitle rows (the German line with the hard words marked, and an English or Chinese line translated on your device) and shows a card at every pause: the hard words with gender, plural and meaning, and the grammar of that line. A Safari extension adds the same rows and card to zdf.de, ardmediathek.de and netflix.com on the iPad.

Frequently asked

What is free and what is paid?
Browsing the library, playing episodes and the German subtitle line are free. The pause card, the translation line, the learning report and the Safari extension are free for 14 days, then a single one-time purchase of PauseCard Full. There is no subscription and nothing is charged automatically when the trial ends.

How do I start the trial?
Pause an episode and tap "Start the free 14-day trial" on the card, or go to Settings › PauseCard Full. Apple asks you to confirm; the trial costs nothing. The 14 days count from that moment, also if you reinstall the app or use a second device with the same Apple ID.

I bought PauseCard Full, but it is locked on my new iPhone or iPad.
Open Settings › PauseCard Full › Restore purchases. The purchase is tied to your Apple ID.

The translation line is empty.
The translation runs on the device with Apple's Translation framework and needs iOS 18 or later. The first time, iOS asks to download the language packs; allow that. In Settings › Subtitles you choose English or Chinese (Simplified). Word meanings on the card are always English.

An episode says it is not available in my country.
ZDF and ARD decide per programme where it may be streamed. The app can only play what the broadcaster offers at your location. The Safari extension works with any episode the website itself plays for you.

How do I turn on the Safari extension?
Open the PauseCard app once, then on the iPad go to Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions, enable PauseCard and allow it for zdf.de, ardmediathek.de and netflix.com. On the iPhone the extension works too, but the translation line arrives after the app has translated the episode.

Where is my learning report?
Turn on learn mode in Settings › Card. Every pause, tapped word and dictionary look-up of an episode is then collected in the Report tab, which exports to HTML, CSV and Anki.

Does the app collect any data?
No. There is no account, no server and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
